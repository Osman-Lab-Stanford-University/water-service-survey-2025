# Questionnaire provenance and reference-edition notes

This repository contains formatted **research reference editions** of the 2025 Household Water Service Survey. The documents do not claim to be a screen-by-screen capture of the original Qualtrics survey. The original source exports and consent materials are retained separately in the investigators' study records.

## Source and presentation

The English questionnaire was reconstructed from a 2025 English Word export associated with Karur. Tamil reference material was checked against Tamil Word exports associated with Salem and Namakkal. Those two Tamil exports have the same substantive numbered questionnaire structure. The question identifiers and response-choice ordering have been retained. Each edition has 106 numbered questions in 15 thematic sections plus the administrative information section.

The source Word exports contain irregular page structure. Pagination, fonts, headings, line spacing and repeated categorical-rating displays were therefore redesigned for compact, readable reference documents. For Q1.2, the six original percentage sliders are represented as a noninteractive 0–100% table with the original 5-point tick increments identified. These PDFs and DOCX files are **not functional electronic survey forms**.

The historical informed-consent section is omitted from both public reference editions. The blank administrative contact and certificate prompts are retained. No completed participant responses or identifiers are included.

The original exports refer to a water-can illustration after Q1.6. The graphic was not recoverable from the supplied Word exports. A note in each reference edition identifies this omission; the water-can question and its response categories remain intact.

## Bilingual presentation and coding

The English and Tamil instruments were intended to retain comparable concepts and response ordering rather than strictly literal translations. The translation was reviewed by Tamil-language readers and the study team. Formal measurement equivalence was not established by this documentation alone.

In the **Tamil reference edition**, the fourth response to Q7.3 is displayed as `அரிதாகவே தெளிவாக இருக்கும் (4)` to convey the intended English category "Rarely clear (4)." The supplied Tamil Word exports contain a negation in that response label. The correction is in the published reference wording only and does not establish exactly what every participant saw during field administration. No response code or historical observation has been changed by the correction.

At Q3.7 and Q15.9, two printed Tamil codes were aligned with the corresponding English choices by **response position** for the reference edition. Their exported labels and the reference display codes are recorded in `response_code_crosswalk.csv`. This is a presentation crosswalk, not a retrospective recoding of Qualtrics responses.

Q6.1 asks about satisfaction with the **current primary water supply source**, not an independent measure of overall water-service satisfaction. Other naturally occurring English–Tamil phrasing differences are preserved.

## Ethics, administration and data protection

The study is identified under Stanford IRB Protocol No. 82168. The historical participant-information materials described voluntary participation, confidential survey administration and safeguards for optional identifying information used for certificate or raffle arrangements. The original consent text is not reproduced in the reference questionnaires.

Fieldwork was conducted in September 2025, primarily in Tamil Nadu, South India, with field activities in Karur, Salem and Namakkal. The study used investigator-coordinated nonprobability convenience sampling, with local partners providing field support and survey logistics. Survey access information was shared through local networks, and the investigator coordinated field activities. Many respondents completed the questionnaire during class time, but participation was not restricted to organized sessions, and individual completion locations were not systematically recorded. Respondents completed the password-protected Qualtrics questionnaire independently after reviewing electronic consent information. Local partners assisted with field support and survey logistics rather than obtaining informed consent, collecting or entering questionnaire responses or accessing identifiable survey data. The study did not implement planned chain-referral sampling; any incidental onward sharing was not systematically monitored.

The associated research publications and reproducibility materials describe quality screening and analytical samples. Those methods and the source Qualtrics logic should be consulted for data analysis rather than inferred from the printed reference copies.

## Archival limitations and attribution

The original programmed Qualtrics QSF and deployed versions would be needed to verify full conditional branching, all instrument version histories and exactly what individual respondents saw. Apart from the documented certificate conditional prompt, branching cannot be comprehensively verified from these Word exports alone.

For citation when reproducing, using or adapting questions, use the associated Research Square study preprint: https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10888037/v1 and identify the GitHub reference release.
